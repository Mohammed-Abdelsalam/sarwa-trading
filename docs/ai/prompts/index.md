# Prompts

The real prompts, plans, decision records and agent briefs used to build this repo, in timeline order. They are working documents, not polished docs: they show what the AI was asked to do, with what constraints, and what was decided along the way.

**How they were edited for the repo.** Files 01–09 and `agent-rules.md` are copied as written. The only changes: local machine paths became repo-relative paths, a private design-canvas link was replaced by a placeholder, a few personal preference lines were removed, and the Arabic copy columns in 03 and 05 were replaced by `[AR]` (the shipped Arabic strings live in `packages/i18n`). Files 10 and 11 were rewritten for the repo. The two briefs are reconstructed from the sessions that used them.

Commit hashes, dates and counts inside the files are as they were at the time.

## Things worth noticing

- **Decisions change, and the record shows it.** 04 (Q2) and 07 say the watchlist requires an account. Re-reading the take-home brief ("star favourites") reversed that: the guest watchlist was built on the device account, and `agent-rules.md` records the new decision. The example brief shows how that change was handed to an agent.
- **Precedence is explicit.** 07 says it wins over 04 and 05. `agent-rules.md` lists what is "already decided (don't reopen)", so workers don't relitigate decisions.
- **Every plan carries its own verification.** 04 marks each finding V or S. 06 and 11 end with the checks that prove nothing was lost.
